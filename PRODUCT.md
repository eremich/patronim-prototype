# Patronim (redesign 2026 prototype)

register: product

**What:** Mobile app for a Tel Aviv company that cleans short-term rental apartments. Property managers order cleanings; patrons (cleaners) clean; quality controllers inspect.

**Who / where:** A property manager on a phone between guests, a cleaner mid-job in a bright apartment with wet hands, an inspector walking between buildings. Daylight, glances, one hand.

**Core idea:** The turnover window (checkout → next guest check-in) is the organizing element. The apartment must be ready before the guest arrives.

**Tone:** Calm, precise, trustworthy. One bold element (the turnover window bar); everything else quiet.

**Source of truth:** `patronim-prototype-brief.md` (tokens, type, radius, copy rules).
